[UAGF Requirement UGR-015 | Domain: DOM-HUMAN | Priority: CRITICAL | Force: Normative]
Obligation: High-risk AI systems must be designed and operated in a way that enables effective human oversight, including the ability for humans to intervene, override, or stop the system when necessary.
Why: Ensure human judgment remains the final authority for consequential AI decisions, preventing full automation of decisions with significant impact.
Applies to: Organizations deploying high-risk AI systems
